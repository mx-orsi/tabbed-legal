Tabbed

Privacy Policy and Beta Terms · Effective October 7, 2026 · Contact: mdorsinger@icloud.com

What Tabbed collects

Only what the app needs to work. Everything below is something you type, take, or do inside the app.

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose a profile picture, attach a receipt photo, or scan a friend's QR code. Nothing is read from your photos or camera at any other time.

How it is used, and who can see it

Your information is used to run the app: show your tabs, deliver friend and round requests, post receipts to your friends' feed, and send the notifications you have turned on. There is no other use.

Visibility

  • Your @handle, profile picture and bio can be seen by any signed-in Tabbed user who finds you by searching, by scanning your QR code, or through a mutual friend.
  • Tabs and rounds are visible to you and the friend they are with.
  • Settlements, receipt photos, likes and comments are visible to both people in the settlement and to the accepted friends of either of them.
  • Tonight status is visible to your accepted friends only.
  • Your email is never shown to anyone in the app.

Nobody can put a debt on your tab without you accepting it first. A round someone else logs against you arrives as a request you accept or decline.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage, and sign-in for Tabbed, on servers in the United States. Access rules are enforced inside the database for every account, so each person can only read what the visibility rules above allow.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications to your phone.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging relays push notifications between the server and Apple. Only your device token and the text of each notification pass through it.

Data is encrypted in transit. Receipt photos are stored in a private bucket and served through links that expire after one hour. The developer has administrative access to the database, as the operator of the service, and commits to reading user content only to investigate a problem you report or a rule violation. Supabase, Apple and Google can see data passing through their systems under their own privacy terms.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. A tonight status stops being shown the moment it lapses and is deleted from the server within about two days.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, Delete account. That removes your profile, picture, bio, friendships, tabs, requests, settlements, receipt photos, comments, likes, status, notification token, and sign-in record right away. Tabs that were with you disappear from your friends' view as well. There is no waiting period and no copy is kept.

If you cannot access the app, email mdorsinger@icloud.com from the address on your account and the deletion will be done by hand.

What Tabbed does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking software, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No sale or rental of your information to anyone, ever.
  • No access to your location, contacts, calendar, or health data.
  • No money. Tabbed records who owes who a round. It does not move money, store payment details, or create an enforceable debt.

Information is only disclosed to the service providers above, or if the law requires it.

Age

Tabbed is about buying rounds of drinks. It is intended for people of legal drinking age where they live and is not directed at anyone under 17. If you believe someone under that age has an account, email the address below and it will be removed.

Your choices and rights

  • See your data. Everything Tabbed holds about you is visible in the app. Settings also has an exportable text summary of your tabs. For a complete copy of your records, email the address below.
  • Correct it. Handle, picture, and bio are editable in Settings.
  • Delete it. Settings, Delete account, as above. Comments or likes you left on a friend's receipt are deleted with your account too.
  • Notifications. Turn them off any time in iPhone Settings. The app keeps working.
  • Ask. For anything else, including requests under privacy laws where you live, email mdorsinger@icloud.com.

  • Be decent. Do not post anything illegal, harassing, hateful, sexual, or that belongs to someone else. Do not impersonate anyone. Content that breaks this can be removed and accounts can be closed without notice.

If someone posts something that breaks these rules, or you want a person removed from your view, email the address above and it will be handled by hand, usually the same day. A report button and a block button are planned before the public release.

  • No liability for the tab itself. Tabbed records what you and your friends tell it. It is not responsible for settling disagreements about who owes what, and it makes no promise that any record is accurate or complete.
